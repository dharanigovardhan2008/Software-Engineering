# Problem Statement

## AgriTwin: Digital Twin Platform for Precision Farming and Crop Health Prediction

### Industry Context

Agriculture is undergoing rapid digital transformation as farmers increasingly adopt smart technologies to improve productivity, sustainability, and resource efficiency. Climate change, unpredictable weather patterns, soil degradation, pest infestations, and inefficient resource utilization have made traditional farming practices less effective. Modern agriculture requires intelligent systems capable of continuously monitoring field conditions, predicting crop health, and assisting farmers in making timely, data-driven decisions.

---

## Problem Statement

Design and develop **AgriTwin**, a Digital Twin Platform for Precision Farming and Crop Health Prediction. The platform should create a virtual representation (Digital Twin) of agricultural fields by integrating real-time and historical data collected from IoT sensors, weather forecasting services, satellite imagery, drones, and farm management records.

The system should continuously monitor environmental and crop-related parameters, including soil moisture, soil nutrients, temperature, humidity, rainfall, crop growth, and field conditions. Using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques, the platform should analyze the collected data to predict crop diseases, identify pest infestations, estimate crop yield, recommend irrigation schedules, and optimize fertilizer usage.

The platform should provide dedicated interfaces for different stakeholders:

- **Farmers** should be able to monitor field conditions, receive real-time alerts, view crop health dashboards, and obtain intelligent recommendations for irrigation, fertilization, and disease prevention.
- **Agricultural Experts** should analyze historical trends, monitor crop performance, and provide expert recommendations to improve farming practices.
- **Administrators** should manage users, farms, IoT devices, system configurations, reports, and overall platform operations.

The system should securely collect, process, and store agricultural data using cloud infrastructure while supporting API integration with external weather services and communication with IoT devices. It should provide real-time analytics, secure authentication, role-based access control, notification services, reporting dashboards, and historical data visualization.

The proposed architecture should be scalable, secure, reliable, highly available, maintainable, and capable of supporting multiple farms across different geographical regions. The system should also be designed to support future enhancements such as advanced AI models, edge computing, blockchain-based traceability, drone integration, and predictive analytics.

---

## Expected Outcome

The AgriTwin platform aims to improve agricultural productivity by enabling precision farming through continuous monitoring and intelligent decision support. The system should reduce water and fertilizer wastage, enable early detection of crop diseases and pest infestations, improve crop yield prediction accuracy, and promote sustainable farming practices. By creating a Digital Twin of agricultural fields, the platform should help farmers make informed decisions, optimize resource utilization, and increase overall farm efficiency while supporting long-term agricultural sustainability.
