AgriTwin

Digital Twin Platform for Precision Farming and Crop Health Prediction

«An intelligent digital agriculture platform that combines IoT, Digital Twin technology, AI/ML, and predictive analytics to enable proactive and sustainable farm management.»

---

Problem Statement

Farmers often rely on manual field inspections and traditional farming practices, which may fail to identify crop health issues before they significantly affect productivity.

Existing precision farming solutions often provide isolated monitoring features without offering an integrated virtual representation of the farm for continuous monitoring and predictive analysis.

Delayed detection of:

- Crop diseases
- Pest infestations
- Irrigation problems
- Nutrient deficiencies
- Environmental stress

can result in reduced crop yields, increased operational costs, and inefficient use of agricultural resources.

Farmers also face difficulties in making informed decisions because agricultural data is often fragmented across multiple sources such as IoT sensors, weather stations, drones, satellite imagery, and historical farm records.

Proposed Solution

AgriTwin addresses these challenges by providing an intelligent Digital Twin platform for precision farming.

The platform creates a virtual representation of agricultural fields by continuously integrating real-time and historical farm data. AI/ML models analyze this data to predict crop health conditions, diseases, pest outbreaks, and potential crop stress.

The system also provides intelligent recommendations for:

- Irrigation management
- Fertilizer application
- Pest management
- Crop health monitoring
- Resource optimization

Through interactive dashboards, predictive analytics, simulations, alerts, and reports, AgriTwin enables farmers to make data-driven and proactive decisions.

---

Objectives

1. Create a Digital Twin representation of agricultural fields.
2. Collect real-time data from IoT sensors, drones, weather stations, and satellite imagery.
3. Predict crop health, diseases, and pest outbreaks using AI/ML models.
4. Monitor soil moisture, nutrient levels, irrigation status, and environmental conditions.
5. Recommend optimized irrigation, fertilization, and pest-management practices.
6. Provide dashboards for farm performance and crop growth monitoring.
7. Generate predictive reports for data-driven agricultural decision-making.
8. Improve resource utilization while promoting sustainable and climate-smart farming.

---

Expected Outcomes
